I am a PhD student at the European School of Molecular Medicine (SEMM) at the European Institute of Oncology (IEO) in Milan, under the supervision of Martin Schaefer. I received my Bachelor’s degree in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Padua, followed by a Master’s degree in Medical Bioinformatics from the University of Verona. During my master’s, I spent an exchange period at the Technical University of Munich, where I collaborated with Nicolas Battich’s lab at Helmholtz Munich to benchmark the VASA-seq technology. I then carried out my master’s thesis in Rosalba Giugno’s group (InfOmics) at the University of Verona, developing a pipeline for the federated analysis of single-cell data. Currently, my research focuses on the computational study of adaptation and convergent evolution in metastasis.
