I am a PhD student at the European School of Molecular Medicine (SEMM) at the European Institute of Oncology (IEO) in Martin Schaefer's lab. 
I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science Engineering from the University of Roma “La Sapienza" and Master's degree in “Bioinformatics for Computational Genomics” (BCG) offered by Politecnico di Milano and Università degli Studi di Milano. My research interests and expertise lie in systems biology and machine learning. My current project focuses on the analysis and integration of multi-omics data to predict response to cancer immunotherapy.
